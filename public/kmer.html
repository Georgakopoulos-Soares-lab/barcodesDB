Optional sequence-level filters can flag or exclude barcodes containing simple short motifs such as homopolymer runs, low-complexity repeats, common restriction enzyme sites, and selected functional-like motifs. These filters do not guarantee functional neutrality and are not organism-specific. For exact k-mer lookup, filters only annotate the queried sequence — they do not alter the present/absent result.

For exact lookup, "Exclude" annotates sequences that would be excluded from candidate outputs but does not hide the lookup result.
