Optional sequence-level filters can flag or exclude barcodes containing simple short motifs such as homopolymer runs, low-complexity repeats, common restriction enzyme sites, and selected functional-like motifs. These filters do not guarantee functional neutrality and are not organism-specific.

For exact k-mer lookup, filters only annotate the queried sequence — they do not alter the present/absent result.
Filter homopolymers runs > N
Detects consecutive runs of the same base (A, C, G, or T) longer than the threshold. e.g., AAAAA (5 A's) fails with max=4.
Filter low-complexity sequences entropy
Shannon entropy H = -sum(p_i·log₂(p_i)) over A/C/G/T. Max = 2.0 (perfectly balanced). Threshold: sequences with H below this are flagged.
Filter common restriction sites 50 sites
All sites below are checked when this filter is enabled. Both forward and reverse-complement strands are scanned.
Flag short functional-like motifs 25 motifs
These are conservative sequence patterns, NOT organism-specific annotations. All motifs below are checked when enabled.
